# Everyday tasks

## Mail and contacts

Select a folder or keyword tab, open a message, and use Compose, Reply, Reply All or Forward. On macOS, double-click opens a message window; Command-N starts a message. Save Draft uses the account's supported custody mode and keeps the composer open on failure.

A send that times out may already have succeeded. Check Sent before explicitly retrying. Use contact sync to reconcile relay contacts with Apple Contacts, granting access only when you want that integration.

## Encrypted mail

Read the message's encryption and signature labels. Server-custody and client-custody accounts have different trust properties. An enrolled client-custody device decrypts locally; pairing alone does not provide the key. In-person key QR exchange verifies a fingerprint when the other device supplies one.

## Lock and local data

Configure Require Unlock to Open and optional app PIN in Security. Wrong-PIN lockout and optional erase settings protect the local profile. Hostile Location Protection removes local caches and enrolled key material; understand its limits and arrange recovery before enabling it.
